## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The side history card opens the full history

The side "Historie" card on a contact page and on an organisation page SHALL end with "Historie bekijken", which
opens the page's sidebar on its History tab.

#### Scenario: DqContact

- **GIVEN** the contact page of Sanne de Vries
- **WHEN** the reader presses "Historie bekijken" in the side Historie card
- **THEN** the sidebar opens on the History tab
