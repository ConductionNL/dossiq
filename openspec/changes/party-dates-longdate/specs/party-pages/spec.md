## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A party's side cards spell the month out

The Historie, Identiteit and Bereikbaar via cards of a contact or organisation page SHALL write dates as day and full
month, with the year only outside the current year.

#### Scenario: DqContact

- **GIVEN** Sanne's record was last changed on 3 October of this year
- **THEN** Historie reads "Laatst gewijzigd op 3 oktober"
