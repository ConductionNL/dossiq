# next-step-button-as-drawn Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The next step button is a label

The case page's stage action "Next step" (`stage-next-step`) SHALL be drawn as its label alone, with no icon, in every stage that offers it.

#### Scenario: DqZaak what-now card

- GIVEN a case in the intake, in progress or review stage
- THEN the what-now card's "Volgende stap" button shows its label and no icon
