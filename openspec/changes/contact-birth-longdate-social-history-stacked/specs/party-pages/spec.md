## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The date of birth is written out and the social history is stacked

The contact page's Contact card SHALL write the date of birth with the month in full. The case page's social side
history SHALL draw stacked rows: the action, then the moment and who under it.

#### Scenario: DqContact

- **GIVEN** Sanne de Vries, born 14 March 1988
- **THEN** the Contact card reads "14 maart 1988"
