## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The party overview shows tickets before the contact moments

The Overview tab of a contact page and of an organisation page SHALL show, in this order, the contact or
organisation card, the cases, the tickets, and the latest contact moments.

#### Scenario: DqContact

- **GIVEN** Sanne de Vries's contact page
- **THEN** the Overview reads Contact, Zaken, Tickets, Laatste contactmomenten
