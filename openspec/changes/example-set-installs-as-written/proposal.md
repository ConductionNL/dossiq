# The Zuiddrecht fragment installs as written

## Why
On 23 Oct the install check on :8080 refused the whole Zuiddrecht set. Four contact moments of Buurtvereniging Oost named cases that the fragment writes further down, and the Afdelingshoofd mandate of Waterland stored `plafond: null` where the schema wants a number. One refusal stops the whole set, so no board got its data.

## What changes
- The four Buurtvereniging Oost contact moments move below the three cases they name.
- The Afdelingshoofd mandate leaves `plafond` out; an absent ceiling means unlimited, as the schema says.
- The group `dossiq-sociaal-domein` gets a member, Fatma Kaya (Jeugdteam), who is not in `dossiq-sensitive`, so an access walkthrough has a social-domain worker without the sensitive grant.
- A test checks the order of references and the declared types of every dossiq object in the fragment.
