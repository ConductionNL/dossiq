# Proposal: lhs-matrix-axis-numbers

## Why

DqHandhaving numbers the LHS matrix axes: "Gedrag 1: welwillend" to "Gedrag 4: crimineel" and "Gevolg A: gering" to
"Gevolg C: ernstig", which is how the advice names a cell ("gedrag 2 en gevolg C"). The matrix said "Gedrag: welwillend"
and "Gevolg: gering", so a row or column could not be named by its number.

## What changes

The matrix widget's rowLabels and columnLabels carry the number or letter (en "Behaviour 1: well-meaning",
"Consequence A: minor"; nl as the board). The axis keys and their order stay register data.

Not changed: the board's fourth column ("Gevolg D: zeer ernstig") and its "Gevolg B: matig" are the matrix data of the
example set, which has three columns (gering, aanzienlijk, ernstig); listed for DQ-DATA.
