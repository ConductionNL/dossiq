# Proposal: example-set-buurtvereniging-oost

## Why

DqOrganisatie draws Buurtvereniging Oost: an association with its chair Marja Verbeek and three cases filed for it:
- Subsidie buurtfeest Oosthaven, in decision with Lars de Koning;
- Evenementenvergunning buurtfeest 2026, closed;
- Melding kapot speeltoestel Oosthavenplein, closed.

It also has its contact moments. The set held no such organisation, so the board was measured against a test record.

## What changes

- A `kvkCompany` for Buurtvereniging Oost and a `brpPerson` for Marja Verbeek, both quoting the cast (openregister `feat/zuiddrecht-cast-buurtoost`).
- Three cases with the association as requester, on case types that ship outside the packages: Subsidieaanvraag, Evenementenvergunning and Melding openbare ruimte.
- Four contact moments naming the association as their contact.

The board's files and pipelinq tickets are not in this change.
