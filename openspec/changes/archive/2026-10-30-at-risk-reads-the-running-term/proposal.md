# Proposal: at-risk-reads-the-running-term

## Why

DqDoorlooptijd "Wettelijke Woo-termijnen" listed three requests where the board lists four: 2026-4864, four days from its
deadline, was measured from its start against its case type's target days, which put it in the "on track" half. A case's
deadline follows its running statutory term; the target is only the type's norm.

## What changes

The at-risk rows measure a case's term from its start to its own deadline when it has one, else its type's target. The
days left are the days to that deadline.
