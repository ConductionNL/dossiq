# Proposal: board-live-conversation-row

## Why

DqZaakContact "Live gesprek" draws each conversation held from the case as one 56px row: the subject and its meta
line on the normal line, the "Afgelopen" badge beside them, centred on both, and the footnote 12px under the list.
Live stacked the badge on the subject's line and set both lines at 1.5, so a row was 72px and the card 17.3px taller
than drawn (r45), which moved "Contactmomenten in het klantbeeld" 34.5px down.

## What changes

- In the board look the live conversation list draws each conversation as one row, 10px above and below, with the
  badge beside the subject and meta line; the meta line is 13px; the footnote sits 12px under the list.

Nextcloud's own look keeps the stacked rows.
