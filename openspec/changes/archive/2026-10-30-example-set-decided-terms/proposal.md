# Proposal: example-set-decided-terms

## Why

DqTermijnen's "Op tijd beslist" and "Op tijd beslist per zaaktype", the quarter line under the quarterly report and
DqDoorlooptijd's norm line read terms that were decided. The Zuiddrecht set held only running terms, so on the demo
every one of them read "—" or nothing.

## What changes

Eight cases that closed in the last days, each with its statutory term completed on the day it closed, over five case
types (Woo-verzoek, omgevingsvergunning, bezwaar, melding openbare ruimte, subsidieaanvraag). One melding was decided
four days late.

## Scope

lib/Settings/example-sets/zuiddrecht.json. Needs a re-install of the set.
