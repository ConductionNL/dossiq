# r21-tenant-history-title

## Why

Board dossiq/DqTenant titles the side card with the organisation's history "Historie", as the contact and
organisation record pages do. The organisation page on this environment used the "History" string, which reads
"Geschiedenis" in Dutch.

## What changes

- The `organisation-side-history` card on `EnvironmentOrganisationDetail` uses "Record history" (nl "Historie").
