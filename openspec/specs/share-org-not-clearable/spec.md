# share-org-not-clearable Specification

## Purpose
What dossiq does for share org not clearable. The requirements were first written by the change `share-org-not-clearable` (archived 2026-10-30 under `openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-30-share-org-not-clearable/`) and cover: The organisation choice has no clear button.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The organisation choice has no clear button

The case share dialog SHALL NOT offer a clear button on its selects.

#### Scenario: a chosen partner
- GIVEN an organisation chosen in the share dialog
- THEN no "Selectie wissen" button shows beside it
