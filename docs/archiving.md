# Archiving

Your workspace is the archive. A case gets its retention period the moment you close it. The documents stay where your staff already use them: in Nextcloud.

## What Dossiq does today

| What | How it works | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| Appraisal and retention period | The result of the case sets the selection list class, and with it the retention period. The rules that ship follow the VNG selection list 2020. | [Case, archiving and retention](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqZaakArchivering) |
| Archiving | You archive the case as its case type prescribes, with a reason. Dossiq first checks that result, decision and publication are done. | [Archive](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqArchiveren) |
| Metadata | Every case and document carries the metadata the archive asks for, following MDTO and TMLO. You fill them in while you work, not afterwards. | [Case, documents](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqZaakDocumenten) |
| Legal hold during objection and appeal | While an objection or appeal runs, Dossiq freezes the case. Nothing is destroyed or transferred until the procedure ends. | [Case, archiving and retention](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqZaakArchivering) |
| Destruction | When the retention period ends, the case lands on a destruction list. Destruction happens after approval and leaves a trail. | [Case, archiving and retention](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqZaakArchivering) |
| Transfer to the e-Depot | Cases kept permanently go to the e-Depot as a package (SIP) with MDTO metadata. You keep a proof of transfer. | [Case, archiving and retention](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqZaakArchivering) |
| Accountability | Every action on the case is in its history, including who viewed it. | [Case, history](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/board.html?id=dossiq/DqZaakHistorie) |

## What we build with Haarlem

Together with the municipality of Haarlem we make Nextcloud itself fit for the archive, in line with the Dutch Archives Act (Archiefwet). The goal: what you save is archived.

That removes a link. Today staff work in one system and the organisation archives in another. When the workspace itself meets the Archives Act, the second step goes away.

This work is in progress. What is finished is in the table above.

## Bring your existing archive

Do you already run an archive or a document management system? Integriq brings the files and their metadata into the workspace. You connect cases from other systems through the ZGW APIs, StUF or CMMN.
