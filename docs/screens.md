# Screens

These are all 72 Dossiq screens. Click a screen to open it at full size on [identity.conduction.nl/screens](https://identity.conduction.nl/screens/).

They are the designs Dossiq is built towards, drawn in the house style of the demo municipality Zuiddrecht. The labels are Dutch. You set your own house style with the NL Design System.
