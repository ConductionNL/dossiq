Deliberate no's recorded in this lane​

  • 2.5 case-team-assignment and 3.5 task-lenses-on-engine-store. The property, the column and the lenses shipped. What is left is one chip whose value nextcloud-vue cannot resolve yet (parties-on-the-case 3.4). A dossiq change would carry no requirement of its own.
  • 3.18 task pane beside the tabs. The pane exists inside the Work tab. Moving it beside the tab strip is a page layout decision per case type, which is row 11.6 and buildiq's page-layout-per-case-type.
  • 11.13 translation management in the UI. Register content is translatable through openregister register-i18n. UI strings stay in l10n by decision C05. Nothing for dossiq to build.
  • Q10.12 effort against something other than a case. humaniq's timesheet books it. dossiq places the leaf on a case and nowhere else, because a case is the only host object dossiq owns.
  • 9.11 task-search-fields (inside remove-casetask). remove-casetask is another lane's open change with 3 tasks left. This lane opens task-search-fields as its own change that depends on it rather than editing it.
